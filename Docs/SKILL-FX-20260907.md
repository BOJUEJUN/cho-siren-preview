# Modest skill VFX pass — 2026-09-07

Requested scope: enhance the pixel-line-looking combat effects slightly, keeping the accepted gameplay and supplied character art.

## Implementation

- Replaced cross-screen action-line coroutines with effects local to the actual caster/recipient. There is no line crossing the dice and reroll controls.
- Skill damage uses pink curved slashes, violet rune rings or golden piercing flashes according to the actor's combat role. Ordinary attacks use a smaller impact sparkle.
- Healing uses rising crosses and a low ring; shields use a cyan shield contour and orbital arc. They are driven by actual positive Heal/Shield events, not merely by the announced cast target. Multi-target and retargeting skills therefore affect the recipients that the simulator really resolved.
- Major slash hits reuse the existing `battle-hit-slash-ai-v1` resource, unedited. Other shapes are code-native UI meshes; no new character art, raster generation or downloaded effects were introduced.
- Lightweight pool capped at 20 active objects; effects last 0.28–0.8 seconds, track recipient positions, respect pause/speed, do not receive pointer events and clear on settlement. No damage, resources, timing, saves or battle balance were changed.
